Questions

Transparency is at the heart of our design process. Find answers to our most common inquiries regarding timelines, investments, and architectural standards.

While every project is unique, a custom high-end residential build typically spans 12 to 18 months. This includes a 3-month design and permitting phase, followed by 9-15 months of meticulous construction. We provide detailed milestone reports every two weeks to keep you fully informed.

We operate on a "No-Surprise" financial model. Before construction begins, we finalize a comprehensive Bill of Quantities (BOQ). Any client-requested changes during construction are processed through a formal Change Order system, ensuring you are aware of the financial impact before any work is executed.

Yes, our "End-to-End Excellence" promise includes the complete management of local municipal approvals, environmental clearances, and utility connections. Our liaison team handles all the bureaucracy so you can focus on the creative vision for your space.

Sustainability is one of our core pillars. We specialize in Net-Zero architecture, integrating solar energy systems, advanced rainwater harvesting, and passive cooling techniques that reduce long-term operational costs while minimizing environmental impact.

Our first consultation is a deep dive into your lifestyle and aspirations. We discuss your spatial needs, stylistic preferences, and investment goals. From this meeting, we develop a high-level feasibility study and a preliminary conceptual mood board.
