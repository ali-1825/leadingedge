Land Development & Site Preparation

We transform raw acreage into build-ready parcels through disciplined planning, civil engineering coordination, and infrastructure execution across Southeast Wisconsin.

Request Consultation Contact Us
Service Overview

Built for Lasting Value

Land development is the foundation upon which every successful construction project rests. LeadingEdge Development LLC provides end-to-end land development services for private landowners, investors, and developers seeking to unlock the full potential of property across Milwaukee and Southeast Wisconsin.

Our approach begins with strategic assessment — analyzing topography, soil composition, drainage patterns, zoning constraints, and market positioning before a single machine breaks ground. This diligence prevents costly mid-project corrections and ensures your development aligns with both regulatory requirements and long-term investment objectives.

From suburban subdivision preparation in Waukesha County to commercial pad sites in the Milwaukee metro, we manage grading, utility installation, stormwater management, road construction, and erosion control with precision. Our civil engineering partnerships and municipal relationships streamline permitting across Wisconsin's diverse jurisdictional landscape.

Sustainable development practices are embedded in our methodology. We design stormwater systems that meet Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources standards, implement erosion controls that protect adjacent properties and waterways, and plan infrastructure that serves future phases without redundant rework.

Whether you hold a single infill lot or a multi-acre parcel poised for phased development, LeadingEdge delivers site conditions that allow builders to proceed with confidence — level pads, connected utilities, approved access, and documentation that satisfies lender and municipality requirements alike.

What We Offer

Comprehensive Capabilities

Every engagement is structured to deliver clarity, quality, and confidence from the first conversation through final delivery.

Site Analysis & Feasibility

We evaluate your property's development potential through topographic surveys, soil testing, zoning review, and utility availability assessment. Our feasibility reports provide clear guidance on what can be built, what approvals are required, and what investment level the project demands.

Master Planning & Layout

Collaborating with civil engineers and planners, we develop site layouts that optimize lot yield, preserve natural features, and meet municipal design standards. Every plan balances economic efficiency with community compatibility and environmental responsibility.

Earthwork & Grading

Precision grading transforms uneven terrain into build-ready pads with proper drainage slopes and compaction standards. Our equipment operators and survey teams work to engineered specifications, ensuring foundations sit on stable, properly prepared ground.

Utility Infrastructure

We install or extend water, sewer, gas, electric, and telecommunications infrastructure to serve individual lots or entire developments. Coordination with utility providers and compliance with Wisconsin Public Service Commission requirements is managed entirely through our team.

Stormwater Management

Engineer and construct detention ponds, bioswales, permeable systems, and conveyance infrastructure that meet Wisconsin stormwater standards. Our designs protect downstream properties while satisfying municipal and WDNR review processes.

Road & Access Construction

Build private drives, subdivision roads, and access improvements that meet Wisconsin Department of Transportation and local municipal specifications. We handle base course installation, paving, curb and gutter, and signage to deliver fully functional access.

Our Process

A Disciplined Path to Excellence

01

Consultation

We meet on-site to walk your property, review any existing surveys or plats, and discuss your development goals — whether preparing a single lot, subdividing acreage, or creating a multi-phase community. This initial assessment identifies regulatory pathways, potential constraints, and the professional team required for your project.

02

Planning

Our team coordinates with civil engineers, surveyors, and environmental consultants to develop a comprehensive development plan. This includes preliminary plat design, utility routing, stormwater calculations, cost estimates, and a permitting schedule aligned with your target construction timeline.

03

Design & Preparation

Final engineering drawings are submitted for municipal and WDNR approval while we prepare erosion control plans, traffic management strategies, and construction logistics. Material procurement and subcontractor mobilization are scheduled to begin immediately upon permit issuance.

04

Execution

Earthwork, utility installation, stormwater construction, and road building proceed in sequenced phases with continuous survey verification. Our site superintendents monitor compaction testing, pipe installation standards, and erosion control compliance throughout active construction.

05

Final Inspection & Delivery

Upon completion, we coordinate final inspections with municipalities and utility providers, obtain certificates of occupancy or acceptance where applicable, and deliver as-built documentation. Your parcels are certified build-ready with all infrastructure tested, connected, and approved.
