Land Development & Site Preparation

We transform raw acreage into build-ready parcels through disciplined planning, civil engineering coordination, and infrastructure execution across Southeast Wisconsin.

Service Overview

Built for Lasting Value

LeadingEdge prepares land for successful builds across Milwaukee and Southeast Wisconsin — from grading and utilities to permitting and site access.

We assess your property early, plan infrastructure properly, and deliver build-ready sites that meet municipal requirements and keep your project moving forward.

What We Offer

Comprehensive Capabilities

Every engagement is structured to deliver clarity, quality, and confidence from the first conversation through final delivery.

Site Analysis & Feasibility

We evaluate your property's development potential through topographic surveys, soil testing, zoning review, and utility availability assessment. Our feasibility reports provide clear guidance on what can be built, what approvals are required, and what investment level the project demands.

Master Planning & Layout

Collaborating with civil engineers and planners, we develop site layouts that optimize lot yield, preserve natural features, and meet municipal design standards. Every plan balances economic efficiency with community compatibility and environmental responsibility.

Earthwork & Grading

Precision grading transforms uneven terrain into build-ready pads with proper drainage slopes and compaction standards. Our equipment operators and survey teams work to engineered specifications, ensuring foundations sit on stable, properly prepared ground.

Utility Infrastructure

We install or extend water, sewer, gas, electric, and telecommunications infrastructure to serve individual lots or entire developments. Coordination with utility providers and compliance with Wisconsin Public Service Commission requirements is managed entirely through our team.

Stormwater Management

Engineer and construct detention ponds, bioswales, permeable systems, and conveyance infrastructure that meet Wisconsin stormwater standards. Our designs protect downstream properties while satisfying municipal and WDNR review processes.

Road & Access Construction

Build private drives, subdivision roads, and access improvements that meet Wisconsin Department of Transportation and local municipal specifications. We handle base course installation, paving, curb and gutter, and signage to deliver fully functional access.
